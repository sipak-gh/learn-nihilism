# References

This file lists the sources behind the claims in this course: dates, attributions, translations, and the readings of each thinker the chapters rely on. Use it to check a claim or to read further. Quotations from public-domain books were checked word for word against the Project Gutenberg texts bundled in [readings/](readings/README.md); the Gutenberg page for each book is listed under the chapters that quote it. Books still in copyright are listed under each chapter's **Books** heading as plain bibliographic entries.

Each online source has two links: **original** (the live page) and **archived** (a fixed copy, in case the original moves or changes). For most pages the archived copy is a snapshot in the Internet Archive's [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/). The Wayback Machine does not return pages from the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, so for every SEP entry the archived link is instead the SEP's own dated archive edition, `https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2026/entries/<slug>/`, which the encyclopedia keeps unchanged and recommends for citation. Four pages have no snapshot anywhere (a publisher's page in Chapter 1 and three Britannica articles in Chapter 4); they are marked "archived: none available."

## Part 3 — A graduate-level path

[Chapter 15](15-beyond-nihilism.md#a-graduate-reading-path) sets out a six-stage path (primary texts, the history of the concept, meaning in life, metaethics, continental and comparative work, research questions). This part adds the reference works and the key secondary literature on each figure, so that you can enter the scholarly conversation on any chapter.

Read the encyclopedia entry before the monographs: it maps the positions and names the scholars who hold them.
